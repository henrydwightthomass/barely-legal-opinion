Why this exists

Politics has a very long expiration date.

Debt. Housing. Education. AI. War. Climate. Civil liberties. The consequences of political decisions can outlive the people who made them.

The Barely Legal Opinion is an attempt to pay attention before those consequences become history.

Principles

I don’t have to agree with you to hear you.

I don’t have to support someone to criticize their opposition.

If the facts change, my opinion can too.

About The Barely Legal Opinion

Barely old enough to vote.
Old enough to have an opinion.

My name is Henry Thomas. I am an 18-year-old high school senior and Kentucky Governor’s Scholar from Louisville, Kentucky. I’m an aspiring public servant and and someone who has realized that many of the decisions being made today may affect my generation the most. I am just entering the electorate, but I will spend much of my life living with the consequences of decisions I had little or no say in making.

Being young doesn’t make me right, and my opinions are highly imperfect. But I have realized that I have a stake in what happens next—and an opinion I feel is worth sharing.

That is why I started The Barely Legal Opinion.

Currently: high school senior, first-time voter, aspiring public servant, and figuring the rest out.

The Barely Legal Opinion is independent political commentary. Views expressed here are personal and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any political party, campaign, school, or employer.
